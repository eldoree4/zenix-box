## Full description
Zenix Box is a mobile AI photo and video editor built for creators who want to retouch portraits, enhance image quality, restore old photos, and create stylized visual content faster and easier.

Features include:
- AI 4K enhancement
- AI Retouch and face enhancer
- AI color improvement and removal tools
- AI styles for anime, cartoons, and avatars
- AI video stabilization and collage tools
- Professional editing controls for photo and video

This app is a prototype and requires product completion, real AI services, privacy disclosure, and final store compliance before production release.
